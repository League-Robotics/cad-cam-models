NEZHA PLUG TAILS REV C - ALL SPARE COPPER CLEARED - ORIGINAL SIX-SLOT CARVERA

PROGRAM: plug-tails-C-clear-4up-cut155.nc (2026-11-11)
Boards 1-2 header tails, 3-4 Qwiic tails. Drawings: gears/view/plug_tail_cam.html,
3D mock: gears/view/plug_tail.html. Made by scripts/make_cam.py, checked by
scripts/check_and_draw.py (validation.json). Not test-cut yet.
The older isolation-only program is in ../archive/rev-C-isolation-only.

1. Single-sided 1.4 mm PCB, COPPER FACING UP, on a flat backer with firm,
   even, full-area tape. Nothing is mirrored.
2. Free area at least 53 mm in X by 30 mm in Y. G54 X0 Y0 at that area's
   lower-left corner, Z0 on the copper. The tongues point +Y.
3. Tools are the standard buildout:
   T4: 30-degree V-bit, 0.2 mm tip.
   T3: 1.0 mm flat end mill.
   T5: 0.8 mm corn cutter.
4. Fresh Auto Z and Auto Leveling over about X2..51, Y2..28 (e.g. 6 x 4
   points, 2 mm lift).
5. The vacuum is switched by the program: M851 S100 after every M3, M852
   after every M5. If you stop the job by hand, turn it off (M852).

OPERATIONS
OP1 T4  Z-0.12 (0.264 wide): two passes round every pad and track, then
        the gaps too narrow for the flat.
OP2 T3  Z-0.10: clears the open copper, edge 0.3 mm from any pad or track.
OP3 T5  outlines to Z-1.55 in 0.25 steps, climb; three 1.5 mm tabs a board
        leaving 0.5 mm, none on the tongue.
12,000 RPM, 300 mm/min, plunges 100 mm/min, rapids at Z5.
About 24 minutes of motion plus probing and two tool changes.

AFTER
File the tab stubs flush and deburr the tongue so it slides into the plug.
Check the tongue pads (0.40 mm gaps) with a loupe and a meter before
soldering.

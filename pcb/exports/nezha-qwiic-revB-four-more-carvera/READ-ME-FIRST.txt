NEZHA / QWIIC REV B - FOUR MORE BOARDS - ORIGINAL CARVERA ATC

PROGRAM: nezha-qwiic-revB-four-more-carvera-cut145.nc
PROJECT: /Volumes/Proj/proj/CAM/nezha-qwiic
DRAWING: cam/panel/panel-layout.pdf
This replaces the archived six-board draft. Four new boards plus your current
Rev B test board gives five usable adapters if that test board passes.
No machine was connected or commanded during preparation.

PLACEMENT AND THE USED BOTTOM ROW
Stock: X150 by Y100 mm, 1.4 mm thick, copper UP.
Reserve the ENTIRE bottom strip X0..150, Y0..40 mm. No new cut or leveling
point enters this strip. This covers the original bottom-row test jobs at
nominal Y15..39 with their kerfs, regardless of their X positions.
The upper machining area must still be intact, flat, evenly supported stock.
Each new finished board is 44 x 24 mm, in the same orientation as the test:
B3: lower-left X15 Y44; B4: X75 Y44.
B5: lower-left X15 Y72; B6: X75 Y72.
The cutter sweeps X14.20..119.80, Y43.20..96.80 mm. Its nearest point is
3.20 mm above the excluded bottom strip. The right strip is left unused.

WORK ORIGIN - IMPORTANT
Restore G54 XY zero to the ORIGINAL lower-left corner of the stock.
Remove the 60 mm X shift used for the second test. Do not zero on a new board
or add the above placement offsets: these coordinates are already in the NC.
If using an anchor, use offsets locating the actual original stock corner.
Set Z0 at the TOP COPPER SURFACE, not the backer or top of the tape.

TOOLS
Slot 2: standard 30-degree V-bit with 0.2 mm flat tip, 3.175 mm shank.
Slot 3: standard 0.8 mm corn bit, 3.175 mm shank; a mill, not a drill.
Use the correct Carvera collars and normal wireless probe holder.
This program uses the original six-slot Carvera automatic tool changer.
It is not for the Carvera Air's manual tool changes.

WORKHOLDING AND PROBING
Use the flat sacrificial backer and evenly supported double-sided tape.
Keep clamps, screws and the locating bracket clear of the dust shoe and probe,
whose travel envelopes are wider than the cutter-only bounds above.
Keep the enclosure shut and dust collection running.
1. Home normally, load the NC and inspect its preview. Check the original G54
   origin and that the full upper machining rectangle is intact supported stock.
2. Enable Auto Z Probe. Select Work Origin and enter probe offsets X37, Y56 mm.
   This is the center of the first NEW board. Do not use the old X37 Y27 point,
   which is inside the already used bottom row.
3. Enable Scan Margin and Auto Leveling for THIS PROGRAM'S machining area.
   Use 12 X points, 7 Y points and 2 mm probing lift, about 9.53 x 8.80 mm spacing.
   Expected tool-center/leveling rectangle: X14.60..119.40, Y43.60..96.40 mm.
   Inspect the yellow probe dots; all must fall on intact supported copper
   ABOVE the bottom row. Do not scan the whole stock or reuse an old height map.
4. Leave the newly measured native leveling active throughout the combined job.
   The NC preserves G54 and leveling; it does not establish either itself.
   Do not apply a second external leveling correction.

CUTTING
All four boards' trace isolation first, then all eight 3 mm mounting holes,
then all four outlines last. T2 is selected once; T3 is selected once.
12,000 RPM; 300 mm/min XY feed; 100 mm/min Z feed.
Isolation: Z-0.08 mm; nominal effective V-bit width 0.242872 mm.
Holes and outlines: final Z-1.45 mm, maximum 0.25 mm stepdown.
The 1.4 mm stock gets ONLY 0.05 mm extra cutting depth, into the adhesive.
The approximately 1 mm tape thickness is NOT added to the cut depth.
Leveling follows the top surface; it does not measure laminate thickness
or tape compression. The same nominal depth as your CUT145 test is retained.
The 3 mm holes are fully pocketed with no loose center slugs.
Each board retains four tabs: 2.5 mm neck width, 0.5 mm remaining thickness.
Clearance Z5; final retraction Z10. Automatic vacuum mode enabled.
Estimated motion time: about 25 minutes, plus probing and tool changes.

AFTER MACHINING
Leave the stock fixed until the complete job finishes. Remove it, sever the
tabs and dress the edges. Deburr and inspect the fine copper isolation cuts.
Use the Rev B assembly drawing and Amphenol 73306-111LF jack. Fit the insulated
1206 zero-ohm resistor for the SCL crossover, then check shorts and continuity
with the actual cable before connecting power. Earlier Rev A boards use a
different jack footprint and are not included in the five-board parts order.

VALIDATION
Offline checks passed for source CAD connectivity and copper preservation,
every translated feed segment, feeds/spindle/clearance, two tool selections,
stock bounds, complete mounting holes, four tabs per board, and exclusion of
the whole bottom row from both cutting and the leveling rectangle.
See validation.json for the file hash, measurements and checks.
Physical machine calibration, tool identity/runout, workholding and probing
must be checked at the machine; this file has not been executed there.

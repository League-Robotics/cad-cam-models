NEZHA / QWIIC REV B - FRESH-STOCK DEPTH TEST

PROGRAM: nezha-qwiic-revB-one-board-carvera-iso120-cut155.nc
Use this for ONE new board on FRESH UNCUT STOCK. The previously cut stock
has been removed or repositioned. This is not a salvage/re-cut program for
those boards; do not try to align it by eye over existing traces.
The existing one-board and four-board CUT145 files remain unchanged.

CHANGES FROM THE PREVIOUS JOB
Copper isolation: 0.08 -> 0.12 mm below top copper (+0.04 mm).
Mounting holes and outer cutout: 1.45 -> 1.55 mm (+0.10 mm).
For nominal 1.4 mm PCB this leaves 0.15 mm penetration below the laminate,
into the adhesive. Tape thickness is NOT added to the cut depth.
Tabs remain four per board, 2.5 mm minimum neck width and 0.5 mm thick.
The nominal V-bit width increases from 0.242872 to 0.264308 mm.
The isolation paths were REGENERATED for this width; this is not simply a
Z-value edit to the previous paths.

WHY THE FAILED RIGHT SIDE NEEDS A SETUP CHECK
The user reports a good second individual board, two good left boards in the
four-board run, and shallow traces AND cutouts on both right boards, worst at
the upper right. These are visual/user reports, not measured depth readings.
A position-dependent problem affecting two different tools suggests stock
support/flatness or an absent/inaccurate height map. It does not prove a cause.
The old isolation and through-cut depths were the same for all four boards.
Extra depth gives a modest margin; it cannot correct a board moving under
cutting loads or soft tape changing thickness during probing.

WORKHOLDING FIRST
Remove old adhesive/debris and establish a flat, rigid backer.
Use thin, firm tape suitable for CNC work, with even support under the complete
machining area. Avoid stacked, overlapped, spongy or loosely bonded tape.
Press the stock down evenly; check especially the far/right corners for lift
or movement under light pressure before probing. Do not force a bowed blank
flat only at its edges and leave the middle unsupported.
Measure PCB thickness if the stock differs from the stated 1.4 mm.
The 1.55 mm cut is based on 1.4 mm material, not on a measured new blank.
Keep clamps/brackets clear of the cutter, probe and larger dust-shoe envelope.

TOOLS / MACHINING
Original six-slot Carvera automatic tool changer.
T2 / slot 2: 30-degree V-bit with 0.2 mm flat tip, 3.175 mm shank.
T3 / slot 3: 0.8 mm corn bit, 3.175 mm shank; not a drill.
Inspect the V-tip for chips/wear, confirm its identity, clean the collet and
use the correct tool collars. No tool or feed increase is required.
Both tools: 12,000 RPM; XY feed 300 mm/min; Z feed 100 mm/min.
Holes and outlines: maximum 0.25 mm stepdown, ending at Z-1.55 mm.
Sequence: isolate copper, pocket both complete 3 mm holes, then cut outline.
Normal clearance Z5; final retraction Z10. Automatic vacuum enabled.
Motion estimate about 7 minutes, excluding probing and automatic tool changes.
Keep the enclosure closed and dust collection operating.

PLACEMENT / FRESH PROBING
Stock: X150 by Y100 mm, 1.4 mm thick, copper UP.
G54 XY0 = lower-left corner of this stock; Z0 = TOP COPPER SURFACE.
Remove any old 60 mm X offset and establish the new stock datum accurately.
One finished board at X15..59, Y15..39, 44 x 24 mm.
Full cutter sweep approximately X14.20..59.80, Y14.20..39.80 mm.
1. Load the file and inspect the placement preview before machining.
2. Enable Auto Z Probe using Work Origin offsets X37, Y27 mm. This point must
   be intact copper on the NEW blank; it is not suitable on the old cut sheet.
3. Enable Auto Leveling as well as Auto Z Probe. Use a NEW height map covering
   this entire file's machining area. A 6 x 4 grid, with 2 mm probing lift,
   gives about 9 x 8 mm spacing, consistent with Makera's PCB guidance.
4. Enable Scan Margin. Check every leveling dot lies on supported intact stock
   and all probe/dust-shoe travel clears clamps. Watch for stock deflection.
5. Leave the new leveling compensation active throughout machining. This NC
   preserves G54/leveling but does not set the datum or generate the map itself.
   Do not apply an additional external leveling correction.

AFTER THE TEST
Confirm isolation exposes laminate consistently around the board, with no
copper bridges. Check continuity and shorts before connecting power.
The outline remains attached ONLY by the four intentional tabs; tabs are not
an incomplete cut. Inspect the rest of the outline before breaking the tabs.
If one side still barely cuts, stop depth escalation and resolve support,
probing/leveling coverage, tool condition and actual material thickness.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
The file passed offline checks for copper preservation, intended net
connectivity, no shorts/opens, complete hole pockets, four tabs, stock bounds,
clearance moves, tool slots, spindle/feed commands and the new depths.
The same nominal layout also passed geometry checks at 0.15 and 0.20 mm
isolation depth. Those are NOT measured machine/process limits or recommended
first settings. The selected next test is 0.12 mm isolation / 1.55 mm through.
No physical result is guaranteed from the geometry check. Secure and probe the
stock first; increase depth alone is not a substitute for sound workholding.

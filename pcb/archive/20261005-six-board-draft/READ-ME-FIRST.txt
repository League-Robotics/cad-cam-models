PROBING IN CARVERA CONTROLLER
1. Inspect the preview and confirm G54 is the ORIGINAL stock corner.
2. Enable Auto Z Probe. Select Work Origin with X33, Y56 mm probe offsets.
   This point is on intact copper in the first new board; do NOT use the old
   single-board probe point X37 Y27, which is now in an existing cutout.
3. Enable Scan Margin and Auto Leveling for THIS program's machining area.
   Use 15 X points, 7 Y points, 2 mm probing lift. Spacing is about 9.77 x 8.8 mm.
   Expected tool-center/leveling rectangle: X10.60..147.40, Y43.60..96.40 mm.
   The entire rectangle is above the existing cutouts. Confirm every preview
   probe point lies on intact supported stock before starting the scan.
   Do NOT scan the whole 150 x 100 mm sheet or reuse a previous height map.
4. Leave the new native leveling compensation active through the full program.
   The G-code preserves G54 and leveling; it does not establish either itself.
   Do not apply a second external leveling correction to the same program.
5. One combined job performs all copper isolation, then all 12 hole pockets,
   then all six outlines. It selects T2 once and T3 once.

CUTTING SETTINGS
Spindle: 12,000 RPM. XY feed: 300 mm/min. Z feed: 100 mm/min.
Isolation: Z-0.08 mm, effective V-bit width 0.242872 mm.
Holes: two completely pocketed 3.00 mm holes per board; no loose center slugs.
Outline and holes: final Z-1.45 mm, maximum stepdown 0.25 mm.
The 1.4 mm PCB gets ONLY 0.05 mm extra depth, into the adhesive.
Tape thickness is not added to cutting depth. Leveling follows the top surface;
it does not measure actual laminate thickness or tape compression.
Four tabs per board: 2.5 mm neck width, 0.5 mm remaining thickness.
Clearance Z5; final retraction Z10. Nominal motion time about 37 minutes,
excluding probing, automatic tool changes and machine acceleration.

AFTER CUTTING
Leave the sheet fixed until machining is complete. Remove the sheet, sever
tabs and dress the edges. Inspect/deburr isolation cuts and check shorts and
continuity. Fit the insulated 1206 zero-ohm resistor before electrical testing.
Use the Rev B assembly drawing and Amphenol 73306-111LF jack.
Do not fit that jack to a Rev A board, which used a different footprint.
